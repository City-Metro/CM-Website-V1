City Metro Rail Lines

A

A Line

Making stops in Downtown Lego City all the way to Santa Mooica, the A Line is the leader of our rail network in Lego City County.

B

B Line

The longer of our two subway lines. It operates from the hills of North Hollowood all the way to our Union Station.

C

C Line

Introducing City Metro’s newest light rail line, the C! It runs as a direct shuttle from Asian Town station to the world famous Lego Theme Park.

D

D Line

The shorter of our two subway lines. It operates from the quiet Heartlake City neighborhood of William/Western, all the way to the busy Union Station.

E

E Line

Along with the A Line, starting out at Union Station, through downtown, and then to the valley of Table Central.

F

F Line (Airport Terminal Connector)

The F Line is the vital link between the two FLX terminals. Operating 24/7, it allows passengers to travel with ease between the two terminals.

K

K Line

The K Line is the vital link between the suburbs of Asia Town and the busy city center of Lego City. Operating since before it was a part of City Metro, the K still operates on its original right of way.

S

Shuttle

City Metro operates various shuttles throughout the year. Visit alerts.citymetro.xyz for more information on temporary shuttles.

City Metro Bus Lines

Line 76

Our only loop route running from Union Station, around the city, and back to Union Station in about 45 minutes.

Line 111

Running from Lego City to Santa Mooica, it is another alternative to taking the A Line. It replaced the G Line.

Line 112

Running from North Hollowood to Santa Mooica, it’s a direct way to get from the movie land to the beaches of Santa Mooica.

Line 113

Running directly from our Airport to Santa Mooica, it is another alternative to the A Line.

Line 114 (LTP Shuttle)

Running throughout most of the day, it’s the easiest way to access Lego Theme Park from City Metro’s Death Star City Rail Station.

Line 115

Running from Asian Town to Santa Mooica, it’s a direct way to get from the suburbs to the beaches of Santa Mooica.

Line 211A/211B

Line 211B and 211A run directly from Lego City Union Station throughout Downtown LC to the airport. At this time Line 211A is discontinued.

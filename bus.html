How to ride the bus

Before you go

  1. Find your route in the Trip Planner.
  2. Check alerts for detours or delays.
  3. Have your card, phone or BrickPass card ready.

On board

  1. Wait at a marked stop.
  2. Board and pay your fare.
  3. Press the stop button before your stop.
  4. Exit through the nearest door.

How to ride

New to City Metro? Here is what to expect.

On the bus

  1. Wait at a marked stop.
  2. Board and pay your fare with a credit or debit card, digital wallet or BrickPass card.
  3. Press the stop button before your stop.
  4. Exit through the nearest door.

On the train

  1. Have your card, phone or BrickPass card ready.
  2. Tap at the turnstile or fare reader.
  3. Board through any open door.
  4. Listen for stop announcements.

Riding courtesy: offer seats to riders who need them, keep aisles and doors clear, and keep noise down.

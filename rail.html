How to ride the train

Before you go

  1. Plan your trip with the Trip Planner.
  2. Check alerts for delays or service changes.
  3. Have your card, phone or BrickPass card ready.

At the station

  1. Tap at the turnstile or fare reader.
  2. Board through any open door.
  3. Listen for stop announcements.
  4. Exit and follow the signs for transfers.

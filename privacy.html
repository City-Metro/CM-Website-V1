City Metro supports WebAuthn and platform authentication technologies.

These may include security keys and built-in device authenticators such as Windows Hello or compatible authentication systems.

City Metro may store information necessary to recognize and authenticate an enrolled credential.

Your device or operating system may independently process biometric information used to unlock a WebAuthn credential.

City Metro does not receive your underlying biometric information merely because you use a biometric-enabled authenticator.

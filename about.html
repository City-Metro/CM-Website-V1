About City Metro

Running 24/7 rail and bus service and rapidly expanding since 2022, City Metro Transit connects all of Lego City County. Our job is to provide safe, reliable service today while building the transit system the county will need tomorrow.

We move Lego City County.

From the hills of North Hollowood to the beaches of Santa Mooica, City Metro brings neighborhoods, jobs, schools and attractions closer together. We run rail and bus service every hour of every day, and we are always working on the next stop.

Our story

2020

City Metro Express

Our roots trace back to 2020 with City Metro Express, the service that proved Lego City County was ready for fast, dependable public transit.

2022

Rapid expansion

We began growing quickly, adding new rail lines, new bus routes and new stations to reach more neighborhoods across the county.

2023

New Website

We launched our very first website, complete with train scheduled arrivals.

2024

New Agency

City Metro and Republic States Railways became subsidiaries managed by the newly created Republic States Railway and Transportation Authority.

2025

Better Websites

City Metro launched citymetro.xyz, a much better website powered by HubSpot.

2026

Better Web Services

City Metro launched many different websites such as the Trip Planner to allow for riders to have a better rider experience.

Today

Always running

City Metro operates rail and bus service 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so you can count on us day or night.

What we run

Rail

Subway and light rail lines linking Downtown, Union Station, the airport, the beaches and the Lego Theme Park.

Bus

Loop, express and shuttle routes that connect neighborhoods and extend the reach of the rail network.

Airport connector

The F Line links the two FLX terminals around the clock.

MyBrickPass

Our fare payment system makes it simple to pay for one-way trips, day passes and week passes.

About City Metro

Running 24/7 rail and bus service and rapidly expanding since 2022, City Metro Transit connects all of Lego City County.

Our story

2020

City Metro Express

Our roots trace back to 2020 with City Metro Express, the service that proved Lego City County was ready for fast, dependable public transit.

2022

Rapid expansion

In 2022 we began growing quickly, adding new rail lines, new bus routes and new stations to reach more neighborhoods across the county.

24/7

Always running

Today City Metro operates rail and bus service 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so you can count on us day or night.

What we run

R

Rail

Subway and light rail lines linking Downtown, Union Station, the airport, the beaches and the Lego Theme Park.

B

Bus

Loop, express and shuttle routes that connect neighborhoods and extend the reach of the rail network.

F

Airport connector

The F Line links the two FLX terminals around the clock.

P

MyBrickPass

Our fare payment system makes it simple to pay for one-way trips, day passes and week passes.

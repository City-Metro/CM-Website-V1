Fare questions

How do I pay?

Use MyBrickPass to load fares and buy passes, then tap at the turnstile or fare reader.

Which services do these fares cover?

Fares apply to City Metro rail and bus lines. Other services, such as Republic State Railways and City MetroLink, may have their own fares.

Where can I get help with my account?

Email hello@citymetro.xyz or call 213-CM-HELP from 8 AM to 5 PM daily.

Fares

City Metro fares are simple. See how much it costs, how to pay, and where to get help.

How much does it cost to ride City Metro?

Regular fare

$1.25

One-way ride on any City Metro rail or bus line.

Day pass

$5

Unlimited rides until the end of the service day.

Week pass

$18

Unlimited rides for seven days.

Ways to pay

Pay your fare using one of these options.

Credit or debit card

Tap your credit or debit card at the turnstile or fare reader.

Digital wallet

Tap your phone or watch using your digital wallet to pay and go.

BrickPass card

Use your BrickPass card to ride. Manage it online in MyBrickPass.

Fares for other services

City Metro fares cover City Metro rail and bus lines only. These services have separate fares that must be purchased separately.

Fare questions

Which services do these fares cover?

Fares apply to City Metro rail and bus lines. City MetroLink, Republic State Railways and City Metro-North Railroad have separate fares that must be purchased separately.

How do I buy a pass?

Sign in to MyBrickPass, choose a day or week pass, and pay. Your pass is added to your account.

Where can I get help with my account?

Email hello@citymetro.xyz or call 213-CM-HELP from 8 AM to 5 PM daily. Limited service on holidays.

What to report in an emergency

Urgent medical care

  • Someone collapses or is unconscious
  • Seizure, severe allergic reaction or trouble breathing
  • Visible injury or heavy bleeding

Criminal activity

  • Assault, threats or harassment
  • Theft or vandalism
  • Weapons or other dangerous items

Safety hazards

  • Fire, smoke or unusual smells
  • Objects or people on the tracks
  • Anything blocking an exit or path

Rider safety tips

Ride with a friend

Travel with others when you can, especially late at night.

Stay alert

Keep your phone, bag and belongings in view and secure.

Give yourself time

Allow enough time to board and exit safely. Never rush the doors.

Know how to report

Save 888-CM-SAFE and text 77777 in your phone before you ride.
